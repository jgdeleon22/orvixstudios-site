Company

About Orvix Studios

Orvix Studios started in a cockpit. Our founder, an airline pilot, was tired of juggling five apps and a spreadsheet to track a single trip, so he built one app that does it all.

That app became Orvix Aero. The same approach now drives everything we make: practical software, designed by people who use it every day.
