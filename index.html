The studio

Started in a cockpit.

Our founder, an airline pilot, was tired of juggling five apps and a spreadsheet to track a single trip, so he built one app that does it all. That app became Orvix Aero.

The same approach now runs through both divisions: practical software, made by people who use it every day, whether that's on a four-day trip or a Friday night online.
