Orvix Cabin

The trip app for flight attendants. Add your schedule from a screenshot and everything else fills itself in.

Ask about itIn testing
Today at a glanceYour next report and each leg, with delays and gate changes in red.
Trip alertsA notification when a leg is delayed, the gate changes or the inbound plane is late.
Duty and restYour duty day and rest under the FAA flight attendant rules, and when you’re legal again.
LayoversWeather, food and errands near the hotel, your notes for each city, and tips from other crews.
Pay estimateThe month’s pay with your hourly rate, rigs, minimum day and per diem.
CrashpadsShare a crashpad with your roommates and see who’s staying which nights.
English and SpanishPick a language in Settings, or let it follow your phone.

Documents, expenses, pay rates and packing lists stay on your phone. No ads. Orvix Cabin never shows airline-internal data such as seat loads, standby lists or crew lists.
