About the studio

Orvix Studios LLC is a Florida software company with two divisions. Orvix Aviation makes apps for airline crews and their families: Orvix Aero, a pilot logbook that fills itself in; Orvix Family, which lets families follow their pilot’s trips; and Orvix Cabin, the trip app for flight attendants. Orvix Gaming makes Orvix Grid, a Star Citizen companion, and Orvix Scripts, FiveM scripts for roleplay servers. The studio was started by an airline pilot.
