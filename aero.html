Orvix Aero

A pilot logbook that fills itself in, with FAR 117 limits, your schedule from a screenshot, commuting and a daily briefing in one app.

App Storeorvixaero.comOn the App Store
Automatic logbookFlights are detected and logged with airports, times and tail number, then added to your totals.
FAR 117 at a glanceDuty and rest limits for the day and the rolling windows, and when you’re legal again.
Schedule from a screenshotTake a screenshot of your airline schedule and your trips fill in.
Commute plannerThe last flight that gets you to base in time, a backup, and live tracking.
Daily briefingWeather, ATIS and what’s next on your trip, in one place.
Your family in the loopInvite the people at home to Orvix Family so they see when you land.

Free to download, with Orvix Aero Pro for the full experience. On iPhone, Android, the web and Windows.
