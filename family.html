Orvix Family

Know where your pilot is, when they land and when they’ll be home: a live flight map, landing alerts, family chat and pickups, shared by your pilot.

Boarding soon on the App Store
Today at a glanceFlying, working, on a layover or home, with a countdown to the next trip.
Live flight mapWhere the plane is right now, its altitude and speed, and the path flown so far.
Landing alerts“Lands in 30 minutes,” “Landed safely,” running late, and made the commute.
Trip calendarEvery trip and layover city, plus your own family events so your pilot sees them too.
Family chatMessages, trip photos and voice notes, just between you and your pilot.
PickupsTap “I’m on my way” for directions to Arrivals; your pilot sees when you’ll get there.
Kids’ modeA simple, big-picture screen for little ones, locked by a parent.

Free, with no ads. Orvix Family needs an invite code from a pilot who uses Orvix Aero; live flights, landing alerts and pickups need the pilot’s Orvix Aero Pro. Your pilot decides who joins and what each person sees.

Questions about Orvix Family? Send us a message.
